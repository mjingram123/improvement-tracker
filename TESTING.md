# QA scenario pass

Run at 375x812 in the in-app browser against http://localhost:8765/ (main branch, `python3 -m http.server 8765 --directory improvement-tracker` from the sandbox root, or the "improve" preview). Run the pass once in light mode and once in dark mode (device/OS dark mode, or the browser's `prefers-color-scheme: dark` emulation) — see item 22.
Before each pass: clear site data (localStorage, IndexedDB) unless the scenario says otherwise. Report each as PASS or FAIL with steps and a screenshot on FAIL.

1. Fresh load: no console errors. Tab bar shows four tabs, in order, Day / Night / Week / More, each with an outline icon in a pill above its label; the current tab's pill and label are in blue (`--blue-soft` pill, `--blue-700` icon/label) with `aria-current="page"`. The floating button is visible above the tab bar, pill-shaped, showing a wave icon and the label "Urge".
2. Default tab: reload the app (or change the device clock) at a few points in the day. Between the rollover hour (More > Schedule > Day ends at, default 4am) and 15:00, it opens on Day. At 15:00 or later, or before the rollover hour, it opens on Night.
3. Day tab: header shows only the weekday and date (30px Outfit), no "morning"/"evening" kicker (T23 decision, kept through the redesign). Stretched toggle persists across reload. The Stretched/Hungover card gets a 2px green border and a green "Morning done" line once Stretched is on and, on hangover-kit days, all four kit chips are checked.
4. Hangover kit appears only on the configured days (fake via `settings.hangoverDays` in localStorage if today is not one of them). Turning it on reveals four kit chips (LMNT, Food, Ibuprofen, Walk) with a check dot; turning it off clears them.
5. Commitments: an uppercase "COMMITMENTS" label sits above a card of check rows. Water polo and Dinner out rows appear only on their configured days; Gym always shows. Checking a row turns its label muted with a strikethrough. All three persist across reload.
6. Setup checklist: on a fresh install it is the top card on Day, titled "Getting set up", with three items (Add to Home Screen, Turn on nudges, First backup) and a "Done, hide this" button; items are tappable and persist. Tapping "Done, hide this" removes the card from Day and the same three items (still tappable, no "Done" button) appear instead in More under "Getting set up".
7. Floating button on every tab: switch through Day, Night, Week, More and confirm the button is visible and tappable on all four, not just one.
8. Night, first visit of the day: opens directly into the 4-step flow at step 1 (Wind-down), with a step-dot indicator (4 dots, completed/current dots stretched and blue) plus a "Step 1 of 4" caption, the step title as the big screen title, a Next button, and a Skip text button (no Back on step 1).
9. Night flow navigation: Next and Skip both advance to the next dot and mark the step visited; tapping a dot jumps straight to that step without losing data. Wind-down's Start 15:00 / countdown / Cancel / done-and-reset behave exactly as before, now inside the centered ring card.
10. Wind-down ring: starting the timer shows a 240px blue-to-green gradient ring (full circle at 15:00) with the mm:ss countdown centered inside it; the ring empties smoothly as the timer counts down, driven by the same `windDown.endsAt` timestamp and the existing 1s `tick()` (no second interval). It keeps ticking correctly off-step and off-tab: start the timer, then move to another Night step or switch to Day/Week/More; forcing `windDown.endsAt` into the past (via localStorage) still flips it to done with a toast on the next tick, wherever you are.
11. Night summary: after Next/Skip through all four steps (or as soon as the existing partial-done rule is met: wind-down done, or any rating, or a non-blank sentence), Night switches to a summary card listing each section's value with a green "Edit" link; skipped sections read "Skipped" in muted gray, never a warning color. The card ends with "Logged for <date>." Reloading the tab mid-evening keeps the same visited/step progress (sessionStorage), but a fresh day resets it.
12. Night summary Edit: tapping a row (or its Edit link) jumps back into that step, shows a "Back to summary" text link, and lets you change the value; using it returns to the summary with the updated value shown.
13. Urge overlay, idle state: tap the floating "Urge" button from any tab. A full-screen overlay opens with the kicker "urge" and a close circle top right; Escape also closes it. Idle state shows the Scrolling/Porn segmented control (selected pill fills blue), a labeled "Trigger" field (placeholder "two words: bored, tired, alone"), a green "Start 10 minutes" button, the Shortcuts timer link when `useShortcutTimers` is on and no ntfy topic is set, and below a divider this week's tally ("N rode · M gave in") and the last four urges (time, kind, trigger, outcome).
14. Urge overlay, running state: the kicker changes to "riding it out"; Start 10 minutes switches to a 260px gradient ring with the mm:ss countdown centered inside (same full-circle-draining behavior as the wind-down ring, driven by `endsAt`), the trigger line, a disabled-until-zero "Rode it out", an always-enabled amber "Gave in", and the line "Wait it out. Rode it out unlocks when the timer ends." Closing and reopening the overlay while a timer runs returns straight to this running state.
15. Floating button while an urge runs: fills solid blue with white text reading "Riding it out · mm:ss", updating every second on whichever tab is showing; tapping it reopens the overlay in the running state. Forcing the urge's `endsAt` into the past (via localStorage) enables "Rode it out" in the overlay and changes the floating button text within a second, without a manual refresh.
16. Urge outcome: tapping "Rode it out" (once unlocked) or "Gave in" (any time) logs the outcome, closes the overlay with a toast ("Rode it out. Logged." / "Logged."), and updates the week tally and last-four list the next time the overlay is opened. "rode it out" renders in green, "gave in" in amber; neither the app nor its manifest/onboarding/ntfy copy ever mentions scrolling or porn outside the overlay/Night flow, and no slip is ever shown in red.
17. Week: no page title, just the chevron/date-range navigator (Outfit 22px). Slip rates show "n of m" with blue progress bars; last-week comparison appears only when last week has data; the sparkline (green, 314x64) renders with gaps for missing days and a muted "–" under days with no rating; Journal lists sentences and italic slip notes, or "Nothing written yet." when empty; prev/next week nav works and next is disabled on the current week.
18. More: Backup/Restore behave as before (Copy puts JSON on the clipboard and updates "Last backup"; Download triggers a file and is hidden in standalone/home-screen mode; Share falls back to Download when unsupported; Restore merges pasted or chosen backups — counts match, existing newer days are not overwritten, junk text shows the error toast). Schedule shows single-letter S M T W T F S day chips (selected fills green) and a 4-option "Day ends at" segmented control (12/2/4/6 am); if a previously saved rollover hour is some other value, a fifth option for that hour appears, selected, and both persist.

Reminders (ntfy, T39): with no ntfy topic set, the "Send test" button is disabled and the Shortcut-timers toggle/links (here and in the Wind-down/Urge screens) are visible; no copy anywhere mentions a Shortcuts automation that opens the app. After typing a topic into "ntfy topic" and leaving the field, "Send test" enables and the Shortcut-timers toggle/links disappear (`useShortcutTimers` keeps its saved value under the hood). With "Timer pings" on and a topic set, starting a wind-down or urge timer fires a silent, fire-and-forget POST (`Title: Timer`, `Delay: 15m` or `10m`, body "Time.") — confirm delivery in the ntfy app or web viewer after the delay; cancelling the wind-down does not recall it. 22. Dark-mode screenshot pass: repeat items 1, 3, 8, 10, 13-14, 17, and 19 with the OS/browser in dark mode.
